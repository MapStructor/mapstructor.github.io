A page with a map in it

test/embed — how a MapStructor map looks inside somebody else's page

This page stands in for any website that wants to show a MapStructor map: a blog post, a museum's site, a school's resource page. The paragraph you are reading is the page's own text; the map below is MapStructor's, placed here with an embed snippet exactly as a site owner would paste it. Nothing on this page talks to MapStructor's database. The map inside the frame is the published copy, so it behaves here exactly as it does at its own address.

Text continues after the map, the way an article would: a caption, a paragraph that refers to what the reader just looked at, a link onward. The point of the test is to see how the frame sits inside flowing text — its height, its edges, whether the map's own header and side panel make sense at this size, and whether the controls still work with the page scrolling around it.

Only the iframe's own attributes are used (src, width, height, style, allow, title, loading). Scripts and everything else are ignored.
